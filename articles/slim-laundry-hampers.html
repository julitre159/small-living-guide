SMALL LAUNDRY

Best Slim Laundry Hampers for Apartments

A narrow hamper can be useful beside a washer, in a bathroom or inside a bedroom. The best size depends on how often you do laundry and where the hamper will live.

Quick takeaway: In a small home, measure first, prioritize access and choose storage around the way you actually use the space.

Measure the gap

Measure the narrowest point where the hamper will sit and check whether nearby doors can still open.

Balance width and capacity

A very slim hamper may need frequent emptying. Choose capacity based on your laundry routine rather than the largest size that fits.

Consider handles and mobility

Handles or wheels can make a full hamper easier to move. Check construction and weight expectations before purchase.

Think about ventilation

For damp items, choose a design and material appropriate for the manufacturer's stated use. Do not leave wet laundry sitting in a closed hamper for extended periods.
